### 대피소 시설 장애인 접근성 설명 (Detail)

![image.png](NYC%20Hurricane%20Evacuation%20Zone%20Finder/image%203.png)

예: **PS 7- this is an accessible facility** (858 Jamaica Avenue) ← 여기서 “**this is an accessible facility”는 웹사이트에서 정보 복사 후 여기 붙여넣으니 나타남!**

- The main/accessible entrance is located at 858 Jamaica Avenue (between Crescent Street and Hemlock Street).
- Access to the main shelter areas will be unobstructed and without steps.
- Accessible restrooms are available.
- Accessible dormitory and eating/cafeteria areas are available.
- Additional amenities will be available, such as accessible cots, storage space for refrigerated medication, power strips for charging mobility and other accessibility devices, and mobility aids (canes, crutches, manual wheelchairs, etc.).
- Auxiliary aids and services will be available, including sign language interpretation, sound amplifiers, and documents in alternative formats. People who are blind or have low vision will be provided physical and verbal guidance to navigate shelter and evacuation center spaces upon request.
