[‎Emergency: Severe Weather App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/emergency-severe-weather-app/id954783878)

The best time to get ready is before a disaster happens. That's why Emergency features step-by-step guides to help you prepare for wildfires, earthquakes, severe weather, and much more. With a dynamic to-do list, you'll know what actions to take to get ready. And if a disaster impacts your location, you can easily find open Red Cross shelters with our interactive map.

Monitor severe weather and protect yourself and your loved ones with notifications, weather maps, and live updates. Receive NOAA weather alerts when you need them most. Emergency enables you to select which alerts are your highest priority and should override Do Not Disturb. Stay safe with the help of Emergency.

Emergency provides alerts for storms, wildfire weather, floods, and more. Get over 40 customizable NOAA weather alerts on your device. Choose how you would like to receive each alert for your home location, live location, and eight additional locations.

Enjoy a local weather app that's accessible and made for everyone. Emergency is free and available in both English and Spanish.

EMERGENCY FEATURES

EMERGENCY ALERTS

· NOAA weather alerts signal severe weather

· Get tornado warnings, storm alerts, flood alerts and more

· Customize alerts and notifications to your needs

NATURAL HAZARD MONITORING

· Stay safe during severe weather and natural hazards impacting your location

· Hurricanes, floods, tornadoes, and more – Emergency has you covered

· Monitor severe weather seamlessly and stay up to date with real-time alerts

LIVE STORM TRACKING

· Emergency tracks storms and severe weather at every moment

· Doppler radar keeps you up to date on storm and weather changes

MORE THAN JUST A WEATHER TRACKER

· Find open Red Cross shelters with our interactive map

· Step-by-step guides help you prepare

· Create personalized plans for wildfires, tornadoes, hurricanes, floods, and earthquakes

· Our free weather app works with your phone's accessibility features for a customized experience

· Emergency is available in both English and Spanish
